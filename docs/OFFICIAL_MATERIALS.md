# Official materials checked during the sprint

Task: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Z4faOPlT1t6NMCuJSKKB8-vkZ2LVzGMR0PO9rtZgSnM/edit

The five mandatory requirements are: all relevant input sources affect orders;
seasonality and sustained demand growth; lost-demand compensation for stockouts;
one-off large/client orders excluded from regular demand; supplier grouping and
per-line explanation. The scenario includes manager review, correction, approval,
and export. Automatic order sending without staff approval is forbidden.

## Participant instructions

https://drive.google.com/file/d/105Rnhzg3Q5tKjfGZIddRqY13kmq_w4r_/view

One case is sufficient. Use an AI agent and the team repository. README must cover
functionality, architecture, setup, reproducible example, data/services and actual
limitations. Push final code, then use the selected case's "Сдать решение" form
on the platform and enter project title and description before the deadline.
A GitHub push alone is not proof of submission through that form.

## Participation

https://drive.google.com/file/d/1GkZG7O9ME2N7BlTZUQ3Ih1l2kstCxbdN/view

Badge scans on exit/return, 60 minutes maximum total personal breaks, remain in
the hackathon area throughout the final hour. Emergency exception: up to five
minutes with steward approval. No seat/sector changes after start. Personal
contribution and remaining until the official finish are required.

## Security

https://drive.google.com/file/d/1iq6W0b4wYYlhWEcqqbJ3TrRVuKuQ9d5S/view

Use the official venue network. Personal hotspots/tethering need organizer
permission. Keep secrets out of repositories and chats. Do not distribute partner
data or send confidential information to external services without authorization.
Keep the repository restricted to team/organizers. No network scanning or security
testing of event infrastructure. At completion, the owner must handle removal of
temporary credentials and provided local data as required by the instructions.

Runtime architecture consequently performs all data processing on the local
computer and does not call external AI services with sales or client data.
